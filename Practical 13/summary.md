# Practical 13 Summary

The human DLX5 protein used in this practical is UniProt entry P56178. It is
289 amino acids long and is mainly located in the nucleus, which fits its role
as a transcription factor.

Using my Python alignment script, human DLX5 and mouse DLX5 gave a BLOSUM62
score of 1490 and had 279 identical amino acids out of 289, giving 96.54%
identity. Human DLX5 compared with the random sequence gave a score of -308
and 4.15% identity. Mouse DLX5 compared with the random sequence gave a score
of -306 and 4.15% identity.

These results show that human and mouse DLX5 are much more closely related to
each other than either is to the random sequence. The high percentage identity
and positive BLOSUM62 score suggest that DLX5 is highly conserved between
human and mouse.
