This practical was one of the more challenging ones because the models were probabilistic,
so the output changed each time the code ran and I had to think carefully about whether the
behaviour still made sense.

I found the basic SIR model easier to understand than the spatial version, because the spatial
model required me to think about neighbours, grids, and repeated plotting over time.

The most useful thing I learned was how to turn a biological idea into a simulation and then
interpret the resulting plots to understand infection spread and recovery.
